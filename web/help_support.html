Help and Support
How can we assist you?
1. General Navigation
Use the bottom bar to switch between Home, Upload, Patients, and Settings easily.
2. Uploading a Scan
Click 'Upload', select an image, fill in patient details, and hit Analyze.
3. AI Confidence Score
The percentage shows how confident the AI is in detecting anomalies. It is a diagnostic aid, not a definitive conclusion.
4. Managing Patients
In the Patients tab, you can view past reports. Click any report to view or permanently delete it.
5. Updating Profile
Navigate to Settings > Profile to view your details. You can change your password via the Settings menu.
