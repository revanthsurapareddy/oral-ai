Privacy and Policy
Your Privacy is our Priority
Please read the following carefully to understand how your data is handled.
1. Data Collection
We only collect necessary patient information and scan images required for accurate AI analysis.
2. Secure Storage
All medical data is securely encrypted and stored following strict compliance guidelines.
3. No Third-Party Sharing
We do not sell, share, or distribute any patient or diagnostic data to third-party marketing companies.
4. Data Deletion
You have the right to request permanent deletion of any saved reports or your entire account at any time.
5. AI Model Training
Uploaded scans may be anonymized and used to improve the diagnostic accuracy of our AI models, unless you opt-out.
6. Transparency
Any changes to our privacy practices will be communicated clearly and require your explicit consent.
